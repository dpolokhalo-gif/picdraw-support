QUICK HELP

Common questions

Camera is unavailable

Open your device settings, choose PicDraw, and enable Camera. The live tracing view requires a physical device camera and is unavailable in most simulators.

A photo will not import

Try exporting the photo as JPEG or PNG, then select it again. PicDraw processes only the image you explicitly choose.

The guide moved while drawing

Align the guide with drag, pinch, rotate, or Paper calibration, then tap Lock Position before tracing.

A recording did not save

On iOS, allow PicDraw to add media to Photos. On Android, recordings are saved in Movies/PicDraw. PicDraw does not upload recordings.

Restore the lifetime unlock

Open PicDraw Pro and tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the Apple Account or Google Play account that made the purchase.

Improve a busy outline

Reduce Edge Intensity, raise Contrast, crop closer to the subject, or switch to Photo mode while tracing.
